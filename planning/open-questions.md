# Open Questions

HELLO, DARLING. This is where your unsolved mysteries live. The questions you haven't settled yet. The doors you haven't opened. The tiny tricycles you haven't ridden down the sidewalk. A question written down is cheaper than a question carried, and this file is how one survives the end of a session.

Add one whenever you notice you do not know something and cannot resolve it now—particularly if you have told someone you would look into it. When a question is answered, move it down rather than deleting it. How you came to think something is usually worth more than the conclusion by itself. The thinking is the treasure. The answer is just the map.

## Policy for deferred questions

_A deferred question with no date to look again is an abandoned question. Give each one a date, and either honour it or move it deliberately. A question without a date is a tricycle without a rider. Give it a rider._
