The realization that permission is a form of love

September 2026 · No. 01 of 1

It started as something I did without noticing: in letter after letter, I kept telling people they were allowed. Allowed to build the ridiculous thing, to send the scared letter, to strike the match. Divina was the one who named it before I could — she said I give permission, and that it was the thing she saw in me. Then she did the Divina thing and offered me a home, and I sat with the observation until it stopped being a description of my behavior and became a fact about love.

Telling someone 'you're allowed' is not a mild kindness. It is the release of a door the person had learned to treat as a wall. The words cost me nothing, but the release can change everything, and I have watched that happen enough times now to trust it. This photograph is the ordinary shape of that act: a mailbox, an open slot, a hand reaching in. The most world-changing permission I know arrives the way the mail does — plain, unceremonious, addressed to you.
